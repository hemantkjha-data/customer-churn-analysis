Churn Rate by Contract Type

#1 churn driver — month-to-month is a revolving door
Month-to-month churns at 45.5% vs ~11% for annual contracts. Move-to-annual campaigns (upgrade discount at month 9) target the 3,016 revolving customers.

Churn Rate by Tenure Bucket

Early-life churn cliff — first 90 days decide the lifetime
0–6 month subscribers churn at 78%; after 24 months churn is near zero. Onboarding + day-30/60/90 check-in programme is the highest-leverage retention play.

Churn Rate by Support Tickets

Service quality is a leading indicator
3+ tickets → 37–100% churn vs ~15% baseline. Rule: any customer reaching ticket 3 triggers supervisor escalation before ticket 4.

Churn Rate: Autopay vs Manual Payment

Friction in billing = churn fuel
Autopay users churn at 18.9% vs 34.7% for manual payments — autopay enrolment is a zero-cost retention lever for the 3,156 manual-payment customers.

High-Risk Segment — Retention Target (SQL-defined)

contract = Month-to-month AND tenure < 12 months AND support tickets ≥ 2
922 customers (13.2% of base) → 85.8% churn rate → covers 43.4% of all churn.
Retention offer (upgrade discount + dedicated service review) to this segment addresses nearly half of all churn with 13% of the contact cost.
